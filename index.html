KWO Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Last Updated: January 2026

Introduction

KWO ("we," "us," "our," or the "App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, password
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, addiction types, recovery goals, faith preferences
  • Health & Wellness Data: Check-in responses, mood tracking, sleep logs, fitness activities, nutrition information, mental health insights
  • Communication Data: Messages with our AI companion, voice recordings, journal entries, time capsule letters
  • Financial Data: Addiction cost estimates, savings calculations (no actual payment information—payments handled by Apple App Store)
  • Media: Photos and videos you choose to share in the app
  • Emergency Contacts: Phone numbers of trusted contacts for crisis support

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, app version, device identifier for push notifications
  • Usage Data: App launch times, features used, check-in frequency, challenge completion rates
  • Technical Data: IP address, timestamps, crash logs (if crash reporting enabled)
  • Push Notification Tokens: For sending check-in reminders and support alerts

1.3 Third-Party Data

  • Apple App Store: Subscription and purchase information
  • OpenAI: Chat messages (for AI companion responses), voice recordings (for transcription)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status and entitlements
  • Supabase: Push notification tokens and device information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide App Services: Account management, authentication, personalized recovery support
  • AI Companion: Deliver personalized AI conversations, mood-adaptive responses, educational addiction content
  • Personalization: Customize your experience based on your addictions, preferences, and progress
  • Crisis Support: Enable emergency features, track patterns, provide proactive support during vulnerable times
  • Analytics: Understand user behavior, improve features, identify technical issues
  • Push Notifications: Send check-in reminders, support alerts, milestone celebrations
  • Accountability Features: Enable partner notifications and support coordination
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and protect user safety

3. How We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell your personal information.

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Third-Party Service Providers:
    • OpenAI: For AI companion chat and voice transcription
    • RevenueCat: For subscription management
    • Supabase: For push notifications and database services
    • Apple: For payment processing (via App Store)
  • Accountability Partners: Only the information YOU choose to share with your selected partners
  • Legal Obligations: When required by law, court order, or to prevent harm

No Advertising or Data Brokering: We do not use your data for targeted advertising, sell it to data brokers, or share it with ad networks.

4. Data Security

  • Encryption: AES-256-GCM encryption for backup data and sensitive information
  • Transit Security: TLS 1.2+ for all data in transit
  • Local Storage: Encrypted AsyncStorage on your device
  • Authentication: JWT tokens with secure refresh flow
  • Access Control: Role-based access control, ownership verification on all endpoints
  • Audit Logging: All sensitive actions logged for security monitoring

Important: While we implement robust security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

5. Your Privacy Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Portability: Export your data in standard formats (JSON, CSV, HTML)
  • Opt-Out: Disable certain data collection (notifications, analytics)

To exercise these rights, use the Data Management features in Settings or contact us at the address below.

6. Data Retention

Active Account: Your data is stored as long as your account is active.

Account Deletion: When you request account deletion:

  • You have a 30-day grace period to cancel the request
  • After 30 days, all personal data is permanently deleted
  • Backup copies are securely destroyed
  • Some data may be retained for legal compliance

7. Children's Privacy

KWO is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information and terminate their account.

8. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites and services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party services, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries other than your country of residence. By using KWO, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your country of residence.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the App constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Right to delete personal information collected
  • Right to opt-out of the "sale" of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights

12. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

  • Right to access, rectify, and erase your data
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

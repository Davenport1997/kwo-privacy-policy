Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and follow the instructions.

How does the AI companion work?

KWO's AI companion provides personalized recovery support and learns about your journey.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. Your data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM encryption, stored securely, and never sold.

Can I delete my account and all my data?

Yes. In Settings, go to Data Management and select "Delete Account." You have a 30-day grace period to cancel.
